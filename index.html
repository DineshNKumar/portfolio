About

I'm a full stack developer based in Agra, India, with five years of experience turning business problems into reliable web software. Most of my work has been on products people use every day to run their business: a B2B pharmaceutical marketplace, a lead management CRM, and multi-tenant SaaS platforms.

I started in frontend development in 2020, building Ionic and Angular apps, and moved steadily towards the backend and architecture. Today I work across the whole stack: React and Next.js on the front, NestJS, Fastify and Node.js on the server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and Redis for data, and Docker with GitHub Actions to ship it.

At WebHopers Infotech I own two flagship products from architecture to deployment and lead a team of developers, QA engineers and designers. I rebuilt the Lead Management System, cutting lead processing time by 60%. I moved Pharmahopers from a monolith to microservices with Kafka and RabbitMQ. I've also cut API response times by 40% through caching, indexing and query tuning.

I like performance work and picking the right tool for the job. That's why I wrote a Rust search service on Tantivy that beat managed options like Algolia and Elasticsearch on both speed and cost. I'm now adding AI to products, with OpenAI-powered features such as summarisation and natural-language search, and I'm studying for an MCA with an AI/ML specialisation.

I'm open to senior full-stack and backend roles, and to select freelance projects. If you have something worth building, get in touch.
